Explainable by construction

This full-stack system combines classical knowledge-based rule evaluation with a RESTful Python + SQLite backend and dynamic DOM disclosure.

01 / Facts, weights, and expandable entities

Each disease has weighted symptom facts and required premises stored in normalized relational tables. Expanding any symptom or disease reveals its underlying weights, clinical guidance, and cross-rule relationships.

02 / Forward rule evaluation

A single pass evaluates every disease rule. All required facts must be present before its implication fires. Ranking also includes partial overlaps; a partial match is not an inferred disease. 

score = 100 × matched weight / total weight
if any required fact is missing: score ×= 0.60

03 / Backward premise checks & optional symptom expansion

Choose a hypothesis and inspect both its missing required premises and its unmatched optional symptoms. Answering unknown premises or toggling optional symptoms recomputes the ranking in real time.

04 / Consistency checks & expandable contradiction pairs

Contradictory inputs block evaluation until corrected. Active contradiction pairs and red-flag safety warning symptoms can be inspected and expanded below.
